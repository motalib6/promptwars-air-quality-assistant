      5. Practical tips

      • Mask: when AQI is above 150, wear a well-fitted N95/KN95 mask outdoors. A cloth mask does not block PM2.5.
      • Best outdoor hours: check the 24-hour outlook above — the cleanest hours are usually late afternoon; early mornings in winter are often the worst in North India.
      • Indoor air: keep windows closed on bad-air days, run an air purifier with a HEPA filter if you have one, and avoid indoor smoke (agarbatti, mosquito coils, frying without ventilation).
      • Exercise: on Unhealthy days, move workouts indoors. If you must go out, keep it short and slow, and stop if you feel breathless or your chest feels tight.
      • Children & elderly: they are affected first — keep their outdoor time short when AQI is above 100, and watch for coughing or wheezing.
      • Check before you go: AQI changes through the day. Search your city here before stepping out.

      This advice is general information, not medical advice. If you have a breathing condition, follow your doctor's guidance.
